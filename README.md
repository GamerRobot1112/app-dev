# The Amazing Spider-man
## Main Character
Peter Parker - An ordinary teenager who gains extraordinary powers.

Superhuman strength
Wall-crawling abilities
Spider-sense
Web-shooting

How It Happened
Peter Parker was a normal high school student
He was bitten by a radioactive spider
He gained superhuman abilities
He became a hero in New York City

Famous Quote
> "With great power comes great responsibility."**

### Why He's a Hero
Peter Parker uses his powers to protect the people of New York City from danger. He fights crime and saves lives every day.
A teenage boy who became an amazing hero!
